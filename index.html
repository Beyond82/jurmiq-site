HOW IT WORKS

From raw files to a recovery decision.

01

Import

Load BOMs, ERP/material-master stock and optional physical stock or part-master mappings.

02

Calculate

CTB calculates the maximum finished quantity supported by every material requirement.

03

Expose blockers

Shortages are mapped to exact manufacturer part numbers with manufacturer safeguards.

04

Recover

Live supplier results are evaluated against MOQ, price breaks and the true recovery target.

CURRENT VALIDATION

42 → 77 units, without buying toward an unreachable 100.

In the current validation case, six blockers were identified. Live supplier checks showed five sourceable blockers. One part still capped the build at 77 units.

Instead of buying every sourceable shortage toward the full 100-unit request, CTB optimized the plan only to the confirmed 77-unit recovery ceiling.

€594.20 → €317.54Same confirmed build result. €276.66 of unnecessary purchasing deferred.

Validation data shown is a test case, not a customer claim. Supplier inventory and pricing are point-in-time and must be rechecked before ordering.

DESIGNED TO FAIL SAFELY

Recovery data must be explicit, not optimistic.

Exact MPN + manufacturer guard

Shared MPN text across manufacturers is never silently accepted.

Unresolved stays unresolved

No-stock, not-found, ambiguous and API-error states remain visible.

No fake alternatives

Alternative parts require engineering approval; CTB does not silently substitute them.
